# Account Deletion

This account deletion policy applies to the Glorius app for mobile devices, operated by Abdurrahman Samgar (the "Service Provider").

## How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Glorius account and all associated data directly within the app:

1. Open the Glorius app and log in.
2. Go to **Profile → Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll to the **Delete Account** section and tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm the deletion and re-enter your password to verify your identity.

Your account is deleted **immediately** upon confirmation — there is no waiting period.

If you are unable to log in to your account, you can request deletion by emailing the Service Provider at **info@trinexo.io** from the email address associated with your account. The Service Provider will verify your identity and delete your account within a reasonable timeframe.

## What Data Is Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- Your account profile (name, email, password)
- Your athlete profile (height, weight, age, gender, body measurements, health/medical notes)
- Your workout and nutrition plans, and all revision history
- Your workout session logs, set logs, and completed-session history
- Your weight and body-measurement tracking history
- Your progress photos (if any were uploaded)
- Your notification settings and registered push tokens
- Your feedback and support messages
- Your subscription and trial status

This data is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.

## Data That May Be Retained

Some data may be retained after account deletion where required by law or by our payment processors:

- **App Store / Google Play transaction records**: Apple and Google retain their own records of in-app purchase transactions independently of Glorius, in accordance with their own policies.
- **Aggregated, anonymized analytics data** that no longer identifies you may be retained indefinitely, consistent with our [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about account deletion, please contact the Service Provider at **info@trinexo.io**.
